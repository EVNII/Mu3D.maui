Remarks

Luma is Y′, not linear-light luminance. For RGB in [0,1], YCbCr differences occupy [-0.5,0.5]; YUV uses distinct U/V scaling. Extended values are preserved. These are floating-point coordinates, without video quantization, limited range or subsampling.
