Remarks

Unconnected inputs retain the surface's constants. Graphs are shared safely by cloned surfaces; replacing a graph resets render accumulation. A graph contains at most 64 unique nodes. Physical bounds are conservative: add an explicit Clamp when an expression may leave a parameter's domain.
