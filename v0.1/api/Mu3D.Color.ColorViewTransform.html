Applies pinned official AgX, Filmic or ACES 2.0 display rendering to scene-linear RGB. Output is unpremultiplied display-linear sRGB, with one equal to ReferenceWhiteNits. Exposure precedes the view; alpha is preserved. No PQ, HLG or sRGB encoding is returned.

ColorViewTransform(ColorViewPreset, ColorSpaceReference, float, float)

Creates a fixed official display preset from any built-in standard linear RGB space. Exposure is limited to [-32,32] stops and reference white to [1,10000] nits. Reference-white normalization does not change the preset's fixed mastering peak or gamut. Exposed RGB must remain within MaximumExposedComponentMagnitude in absolute value.
