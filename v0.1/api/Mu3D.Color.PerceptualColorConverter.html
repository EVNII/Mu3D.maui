Remarks

These transforms preserve unpremultiplied alpha and extended color values; they do not clip, gamut map, tone map or apply a display transfer function. Lab uses D50 xy = (0.3457, 0.3585), consistently with StandardLinearRgbConverter. Oklab uses linear sRGB/D65 and signed cube roots. Finite coordinates whose conversion exceeds FP32 range fail explicitly.
