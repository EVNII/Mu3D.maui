Remarks

Commands use FP32 world and camera state. Rotation is expressed in radians, pan in fractions of the visible viewport span at the target, and dolly as a logarithmic distance delta. The controller does not acquire raw input, select scene objects, create a timer, or own the camera or optional frame requester. It is not thread-safe.
