Remarks

The behavior hit-tests only the assigned gizmo's own handles, acquires an optional gizmo-priority control lease, captures one accepted native pointer, and maps cumulative drags to the gizmo's translate, rotate, or scale contract. It borrows the scene view, gizmo, optional hit tester, and optional arbiter. It does not choose scene targets, rank scene objects, create undo commands, define pen semantics, or render handles. Presses are ignored unless the attached scene view has a perspective camera, a positive physical surface size, and a visible target belonging to that scene whose own layers intersect the camera layers. Invalidating that eligibility cancels an active edit and releases capture without clearing the borrowed target. Scene mutations remain application-owned; invalidate the scene to observe them at the next presented-frame boundary.
