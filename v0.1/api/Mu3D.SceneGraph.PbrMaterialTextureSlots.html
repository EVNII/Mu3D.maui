Identifies optional sampled texture slots compiled for a PBR material.

[Flags]
public enum PbrMaterialTextureSlots

Fields

All = BaseColor | Normal | OcclusionRoughnessMetallic | Emissive | Clearcoat | Anisotropy | TransmissionVolume | Sheen | Iridescence | Specular | DiffuseTransmission

Enables every currently supported sampled material slot.

Anisotropy = 32

Compiles the anisotropy direction/strength texture slot.

BaseColor = 1

Compiles the base-color texture slot.

Clearcoat = 16

Compiles clearcoat factor, roughness and independent-normal texture slots.

DiffuseTransmission = 1024

Compiles diffuse-transmission factor and color texture slots.

Emissive = 8

Compiles the emissive color texture slot.

Iridescence = 256

Compiles iridescence-factor and thin-film-thickness texture slots.

None = 0

Compiles no sampled material textures; authored scalar factors remain available.

Normal = 2

Compiles the tangent-space base-normal texture slot.

OcclusionRoughnessMetallic = 4

Compiles the packed occlusion/roughness/metallic texture slot.

Sheen = 128

Compiles sheen color and roughness texture slots.

Specular = 512

Compiles dielectric specular-strength and specular-color texture slots.

TransmissionVolume = 64

Compiles transmission-factor and volume-thickness texture slots.
