Remarks

Movement uses world units: local X strafes right, local Y follows WorldUp, and local Z moves along the camera's forward direction. Look deltas are yaw and pitch radians. The controller borrows its camera and frame requester, acquires no raw input, owns no timer, and is not thread-safe.

Remarks

Both components are validated before mutation and produce at most one pose assignment, change notification and frame request. Movement follows the orientation after applying the supplied look delta, which keeps simultaneous held-key movement and look deterministic. With damping enabled, the validated components are queued and consumed by Update(float).
