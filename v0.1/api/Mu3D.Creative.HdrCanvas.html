Stores a sparse HDR canvas with FP32 compositing and explicit working, compositing and storage policy. Public pixels are straight-alpha linear colors; tiles store premultiplied linear color.

Remarks

Instances are not thread-safe. Mutations validate and stage changes before committing; invalid colors, numeric overflow and configured allocation-budget failures leave pixels, revision and dirty regions unchanged. Fully transparent pixels are canonical transparent black. No display, gamut, tone-map, input, undo, gesture or stroke-spacing policy is applied.
